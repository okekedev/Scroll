About Scroll

Scroll is a quiet place to read the Bible. Open any chapter, pick the translation you like, save what you want to come back to, and share a verse with a link that opens right on it.

It began as an iPhone app built around the Fruits of the Spirit in Galatians 5: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control. The home page still starts there. Scroll is free on the App Store and on Google Play.

There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Anything you save stays on your device.
