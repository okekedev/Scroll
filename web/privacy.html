Privacy

This covers the Scroll website and the Scroll apps for iPhone, iPad and Android. Scroll is made by Okeke LLC.

What Scroll collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Scroll does not ask for your name, email, contacts or location.

What stays on your device

Verses and chapters you save, the translation you last used, and your text size and theme are stored only on your device (in the app, or in this browser for the website). They are never sent to us. Deleting the app or clearing this site’s data in your browser removes them.

Where Bible text comes from

When you open a chapter or search, Scroll requests the text from bolls.life, a public Bible service. Like any website, that service and our web host (Microsoft Azure) can see technical details of the request, such as your IP address and which chapter was requested. Scroll adds no identifiers to these requests.

Sharing

When you share or copy a verse, Scroll hands the text and a link to the app you choose. Nothing is shared unless you do it.

Children

Scroll is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.
