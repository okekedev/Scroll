## Description

Discover Bible verses about the Fruits of the Spirit — read to you by a warm, natural voice.

**Fresh in 2.0:**
• On-device narration by Benjamin (American) or Bellingham (British) — no internet required, no subscription, no cost.
• Save entire chapters, not just verses.
• Jump from verse to full chapter with a tap.
• Read continuously from one chapter to the next with the new chapter navigation.
• Manual refresh — no more accidental pull-to-refresh.

**Explore nine virtues:**
Love · Joy · Peace · Patience · Kindness · Goodness · Faith · Gentleness · Self-Control

For each virtue, browse a rotating list of scripture across NIV, KJV, ESV, NASB, and NLT. Save favorites. Read a verse in its full chapter context. Share what moves you.

**Private and free:**
No account. No tracking. No ads. Everything happens on your device.

## What's New (v2.0 release notes, 4000 chars)

New in Scroll 2.0:

Benjamin and Bellingham — two on-device narrator voices for reading Scripture aloud. No internet, no subscription. Everything runs on your iPhone.

Save entire chapters, not just individual verses. A new "Saved Chapters" section on the home screen keeps them at your fingertips.

Tap any verse to jump straight into the full chapter. Then walk verse-by-verse or chapter-by-chapter with the new prev/next arrows — from Genesis 1 through Revelation 22.

Small polish:
• Refresh button in place of pull-to-refresh (no more accidental reloads).
• Continuous reading with zero pauses between verses.
• Cleaner voice picker.

Free, private, and yours to keep.
